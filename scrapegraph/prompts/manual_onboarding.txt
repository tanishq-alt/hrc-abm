- PDF new hire packets hosted directly on company websites
- Pages mentioning "new hire packet", "new employee packet", "first day paperwork", "bring completed forms"
- HR pages with downloadable W-4, I-9, direct deposit, and benefits enrollment forms (not digital)
- Job postings that say the HR coordinator "prepares new hire packets" or "processes new hire paperwork"
- School districts, government agencies, nonprofits, home care agencies, small hospitals

For each company, extract:
- company_name: The organization's name
- domain: Their website domain (e.g., "company.org")
- evidence_quote: The exact sentence or phrase that shows manual onboarding (max 300 chars)
- signal_type: One of [paper_packets, fax_mail, in_person_paperwork, physical_files, manual_data_entry]
- industry: e.g., Education, Healthcare, Government, Nonprofit, Home Care
- state: US state abbreviation (e.g., TX, CA, NY)
- confidence: High or Medium
- outreach_angle: One sentence on how to pitch this prospect

Return as a JSON array of objects. Only include companies with clear, explicit evidence — not speculation.
