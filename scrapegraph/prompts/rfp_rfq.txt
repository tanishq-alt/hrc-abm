Extract a list of organizations that have posted active Requests for Proposal (RFP) or Requests for Quote (RFQ) for HR software, HRIS, onboarding systems, or payroll platforms.

Look for these signals:
- Pages containing "Request for Proposal", "RFP", "RFQ", "ITB", "Solicitation" + HR software keywords
- Government procurement portals (sam.gov, state .gov sites, county/city sites)
- School district procurement pages mentioning HRIS, payroll, or onboarding software
- Hospital/health system RFPs for workforce management or HR systems
- Nonprofit RFPs for HR technology

For each RFP, extract:
- organization_name: The issuing organization's name
- domain: Their website domain
- rfp_title: Title of the RFP/solicitation
- rfp_number: The RFP/solicitation number if visible
- hr_software_type: What they're seeking (e.g., "HRIS", "Payroll System", "Onboarding Platform")
- submission_deadline: Deadline date if mentioned
- state: US state
- industry: e.g., Government, Education, Healthcare, Nonprofit
- evidence_quote: The exact sentence describing what they want (max 300 chars)
- evidence_link: URL of the RFP page

Return as a JSON array of objects. Only include active RFPs — not archived or awarded ones.
