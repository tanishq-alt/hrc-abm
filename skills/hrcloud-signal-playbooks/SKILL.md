# HR Cloud High-Intent Signal Playbooks

## Rule

A visible event is not automatically a buying signal.

Every play must prove this sentence:

> This company belongs because the evidence shows **X**, which likely creates
> **Y** for **Z**, and HR Cloud can credibly address that workflow.

## Play 1: Manual or Paper-Based Onboarding

### Thesis

Companies using printed, faxed, emailed, in-person, or separately downloaded
new-hire forms may have a current onboarding workflow HR Cloud Onboard and Maya
can improve.

### Strong evidence

- company-hosted current new-hire packet;
- instruction to print, sign, fax, email, or bring forms;
- separate downloadable onboarding forms;
- current handbook describing manual steps;
- official job posting requiring manual onboarding administration;
- employee instructions split across pages or systems.

### Sources

- company PDFs;
- employee resources;
- careers pages;
- HR forms pages;
- district and municipal handbooks;
- public board packets;
- official onboarding instructions.

### False positives

- packet is an archive;
- packet is for volunteers or contractors only;
- paper option exists but digital onboarding is primary;
- form is legally printable but workflow is digital;
- source belongs to another entity;
- packet is older than the current HR platform.

### Freshness

Reverify within 180 days and search for a newer process.

### Product mapping

- Onboard;
- Maya;
- People;
- Train where training documents are involved.

### Upgrade to high intent

Add one:

- hiring surge;
- new HR leader;
- expansion;
- multi-location complexity;
- explicit manager follow-up burden;
- active procurement.

## Play 2: Confirmed ADP User

### Thesis

Verified ADP users may need recruiting, onboarding, communication, credential,
or workflow extensions while keeping payroll in place.

### Strong evidence

- company careers or HR page names ADP;
- employer-hosted guide names ADP Workforce Now or RUN;
- official integration case study;
- current job posting tied to operating ADP;
- employee login or support instructions on company domain;
- procurement document requiring ADP integration.

### Evidence grades

- Confirmed production use: company or official source.
- Probable use: multiple current official job postings.
- Exposure only: one job posting or employee skill.
- Unverified: third-party technology database only.

### False positives

- candidate needs ADP experience for client work;
- company used ADP in the past;
- consultant implements ADP for others;
- payroll provider belongs to a subsidiary;
- one scraped technographic signal is stale.

### Product mapping

- Onboard;
- Recruit;
- People;
- Workmates;
- Time Off;
- other approved ADP-connected workflows.

### Messaging constraint

ADP is a partner. Do not frame ADP as broken or obsolete.

### Modern HRIS rule

Verified ADP can increase relevance. It is not a negative score by default.

## Play 3: Active RFP or Procurement

### Thesis

An active solicitation creates explicit timing and buying authority.

### Strong evidence

- official RFP;
- request for information;
- invitation to bid;
- procurement agenda;
- approved budget and active solicitation;
- amendment extending deadline.

### Required fields

- issuer;
- solicitation number;
- title;
- scope;
- deadline;
- status;
- contact;
- budget if public;
- required integrations;
- implementation timing;
- mandatory qualifications.

### False positives

- awarded;
- cancelled;
- archived;
- deadline passed;
- unrelated payroll-only scope;
- issuer not in ICP;
- reseller-only opportunity;
- no path for HR Cloud eligibility.

### Product mapping

Map to the stated scope.

### Routing

Procurement play, not generic cold outbound.

## Play 4: Hiring Surge or New HR Leader

### Thesis

Rapid hiring creates onboarding pressure. New HR leadership creates a temporary
window to standardize systems and workflows.

### Strong evidence

- multiple current job postings;
- hiring velocity across locations;
- first HRIS, onboarding, recruiting, training, or credential role;
- public HR-leader appointment;
- explicit growth initiative.

### False positives

- duplicated aggregator postings;
- evergreen jobs;
- staffing agency listings;
- one replacement role;
- old appointment;
- hiring unrelated to workforce scale.

### Freshness

- hiring cluster: 30 to 60 days;
- leadership move: best at 14 to 45 days, useful up to 90.

### Product mapping

Based on role cluster:

- recruiters: Recruit;
- onboarding roles: Onboard/Maya;
- credential roles: Credential Tracking;
- internal communication roles: Workmates;
- L&D roles: Train.

## Play 5: Expansion, New Location, Plant, Clinic, School, or Acquisition

### Thesis

Expansion creates standardization, data handoff, training, credential, and
communication complexity.

### Strong evidence

- official opening;
- acquisition close;
- new facility;
- regional launch;
- public headcount expansion;
- integration initiative.

### False positives

- marketing announcement only;
- planned but cancelled location;
- franchise expansion with no centralized HR;
- facility has independent buying authority outside parent;
- acquisition too early for workflow decisions.

### Freshness

Usually 30 to 120 days, depending on event.

### Product mapping

- Onboard/Maya for hiring;
- Workmates for distributed communication;
- People for records;
- Train/Credentials for readiness;
- Assets for equipment handoff.

## Play 6: Credential, License, Certification, or Training Complexity

### Thesis

Organizations hiring regulated or skilled employees may need one workflow for
collection, expiration, renewal, readiness, and audit evidence.

### Strong evidence

- multiple roles requiring licenses;
- company instructions for credential submission;
- renewal policy;
- public audit requirement;
- multi-state operation;
- role scheduling depends on qualification;
- credentialing or compliance hiring.

### False positives

- license is required but managed by a modern system;
- only one executive license;
- license belongs to contractors;
- state portal record does not prove manual tracking;
- company is a credentialing vendor rather than an employer.

### Product mapping

- Healthcare Credential Tracking;
- Onboard;
- Train;
- People;
- Shift Planner when readiness affects assignments.

## Play 7: Competitor Dissatisfaction or Workflow Gap

### Thesis

Current users of another platform become relevant only when dissatisfaction,
renewal timing, missing functionality, or a workflow gap is verified.

### Strong evidence

- identifiable recent negative review;
- public request for alternatives;
- integration failure;
- explicit renewal timing;
- RFP replacing current system;
- job post for migration.

### False positives

- anonymous complaint;
- review is years old;
- author no longer works there;
- complaint is unrelated to HR Cloud;
- competitor mention is only in a comparison article;
- no company identity.

### Product mapping

Match the exact gap. Do not attack the competitor.

## Play 8: Frontline Communication and Engagement Gap

### Thesis

Distributed employees without corporate email may struggle to receive,
acknowledge, and respond to company communication.

### Strong evidence

- frontline-heavy workforce;
- multiple locations;
- employee app or communication role hiring;
- public communication breakdown;
- mandatory policy acknowledgement process;
- recognition or engagement initiative;
- merger creating disconnected teams.

### False positives

- office workforce with mature intranet;
- consumer mobile app mistaken for employee communication;
- employee count is high but centralized;
- engagement initiative has no operational buyer.

### Product mapping

Workmates.

## Play 9: Closed-Won Lookalike

### Thesis

Every recent win provides a high-quality account profile that can be replicated.

### Method

Extract:

- vertical;
- size;
- geography;
- workforce model;
- technology;
- use case;
- champion persona;
- trigger;
- deal timing;
- objections;
- proof required.

Find lookalikes that match more than industry.

### False positives

- same vertical but different workforce;
- wrong size;
- no similar pain;
- already in CRM;
- current customer;
- contact at wrong seniority.

### Routing

Anonymize the source win in outreach context unless customer naming is
approved.

## Play 10: Champion or Heavy User Move

### Thesis

A proven user or buyer joining a new company is one of the strongest B2B
signals.

### Required proof

- origin company was a customer or the person had material usage;
- live current-employer verification;
- origin company appears in the person's work history;
- destination is not another customer, competitor, or gated entity;
- destination passes ICP.

### False positives

- same-name profile;
- advisor or fractional role;
- former free-trial contact with no value realized;
- cached job data;
- headline differs from experience section;
- person joined a tiny self-serve company.

### Freshness

Detect monthly. Reach out after the person settles, usually days 14 to 45.

## Signal Stacking

Examples:

- manual packet + hiring surge;
- ADP confirmed + recruiting/onboarding role;
- new HR leader + facility expansion;
- credential requirements + multi-state hiring;
- RFP + incumbent dissatisfaction;
- champion move + destination hiring;
- Workmates fit + acquisition.

Stacking improves intent only when signals are independent or complementary.
Do not count duplicate copies of the same event.

## Signal Upgrade Rule

A low or medium signal may upgrade only when:

- the new signal adds a different dimension;
- both signals are current;
- identity matches;
- neither is contradicted;
- the combined workflow implication is clear.

## Hard Rules

- Every play defines false positives before scaling.
- Every play has a freshness window.
- Every play maps to an HR Cloud product.
- Every play retains exact evidence.
- No play treats industry fit as intent.
- No play treats one keyword as proof.
